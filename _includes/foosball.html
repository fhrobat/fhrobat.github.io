How to play

You (black) against Francesco (blue). First to 3 goals wins; you can change this below the table.

Like at a real table: four rods but only two hands. Your hands hold either the defense (goalie and defenders) or the attack (midfield and forwards), and you switch between the two.

Left handW S moveD shoot, hold to stopA pass sideways
Right hand↑ ↓ move→ shoot, hold to stop← pass sideways
ThumbSpace switch defense / attack

Switching between defense and attack:

With a mouse or a touch screen, drag near one of your two active rods (the darker ones) to move it, tap to shoot, press and hold to stop.

Rules: only a slow ball in front of your players can be stopped; you can hold it for at most 4 seconds, then it is shot by itself. A ball that stops where nobody can reach it is served again. Each corner has a large triangular ramp, from the goal post to the defenders' rod, that rolls the ball back into play.
