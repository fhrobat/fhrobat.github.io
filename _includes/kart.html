How to play

Three laps of the Meadow Ring against a ghost: the recording of a race driven in this same game. Below the track you can pick an easy, medium or hard ghost, or mine, Francesco's. It runs next to you in blue and see-through, so you cannot bump into it, only beat it.

You hardly ever need to brake: drift instead. Press the drift key to hop and keep it held while you steer into the corner; the kart slides outwards round a wide arc. Steer into the corner to tighten it, counter-steer to open it almost straight; the arc follows the steering gradually, so short taps fine-tune it. Steer into the corner to charge faster, then let go for a mini-turbo: grey sparks, then black, blue and blue-and-white for longer and longer boosts.

Rocket start: accelerate as the 2 of the countdown fades, not before. The blue arrows on the track give a boost, you have three turbos to fire when you like, and while boosting the grass does not slow you down. A game controller works too.

On a touch screen the kart accelerates by itself: steer with the pad on the left, brake, drift and turbo with the buttons on the right. Full screen, with the phone sideways, works best.

The best times below show only first names from a fixed list: common English and Italian names, plus those of the people at the Oxford Mathematical Institute. Leave it empty to stay anonymous. You can change it any time below the track.

Recording mode. Drive a full race; once you cross the line for the third time, save it as a ghost and put the file in assets/kart/ of the site. Nothing goes to the leaderboard.
